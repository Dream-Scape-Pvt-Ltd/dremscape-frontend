# Dreamscape Treks — 15 s showreel

120 BPM · 30 beats · 1 beat = 0.5 s · 1920×1080 · 60 fps · built from `window.seek(t)`.

## What the research found (nothing below is invented)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Product** | Dreamscape Treks — Kathmandu (Thamel) trekking operator, "Since 2005", tagline *Himalaya & Beyond*. Personally planned Himalayan journeys: trekking, peak climbing, expeditions, tours, tailor-made trips. |
| **Audience** | Travellers choosing a Nepal route (finder + regions) who then send an inquiry; the site is an inquiry flow, not a checkout ("This is an inquiry, not a confirmed booking"). |
| **Real interface used** | Hero eyebrow and headline, journey finder (Destination / Difficulty / Budget → **Find my trek**), header + Treks menu (six regions), tailor-made page ("Make the trail your own." + 3 steps), inquiry form, success state ("Your inquiry is with the team."). |
| **Brand** | Plus Jakarta Sans only (800, tight tracking) · forest `#153d2f` · deep `#092419` · paper `#f9faf8` · one gold `#e4c98a` (the site's own accent on dark). Logo = mountain with a pine in its negative space, traced from `design-assets/logo-export` to vector. |
| **Photos** | Only the 2048×1536 field photographs in `public/images/trek-memories/` (`home-slide-1`, `home-slide-3`, `sunlit-summits`) — byte-for-byte copies, never upscaled. The Figma region cards are 512×279 and were not used. |
| **Not used** | No prices, ratings, trip counts, review quotes or departures (the catalogue is empty without the backend and departures are labelled *example only*). |

## Through-line object: the **Trail Mark**

The short gold dash that opens every eyebrow label on the site (`— LOCAL JOURNEYS · SINCE 2005`). It is the only gold on screen except the key word of the headline (*one?*, *own.*, *journey*).
