# One Trail: storyboard

**Concept.** One gold line is every object in the film: the ridge of the logo, the dotted trail, a card's edge, a chart line, a wipe, a ribbon. Each shot is that line becoming the next shape.

**Rules held.** One typeface (Plus Jakarta Sans 800). Palette: forest `#153d2f`, gold `#e4c98a`, mist `#b9cfc5`, plus black and white. One camera language: a dolly in the plane, three push-throughs (the O, the chart, the A) and one orbit. No cuts, fades, glows, particles, shake or RGB split. 120 BPM, a kick on every beat, a hit on every bar line, a riser into the last full bar (bar 7). Frame 0 equals frame 15.

**Copy used (all from the site).** *Beyond the Horizon.*, *Local journeys · Since 2005*, *Rooted in Nepal.*, *Himalaya & Beyond*, DREAMSCAPE TREKS. The chart line carries no figures; it is a shape, not data.
